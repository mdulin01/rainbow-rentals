1Getting started

Go to rainbowrentals.app and tap Sign in with Google. Use your own Google account — it's already on the approved list, so you'll land right on the app. Nobody else can get in.

2Your monthly routine

The app opens on ✅ Action Items, and the top of that page is 📣 Rent Day: every occupied property for the month, marked Paid / Partial / Due / Late, with the tenants underneath. Rent collection is a short loop you run a few times a month:

  • 1Around the 1st: look at Rent Day. Anything not green is waiting on a payment. Reminders go out on their own if automatic reminders are on; otherwise tap 💬 next to a tenant to send one from your phone.
  • 2When money lands: tap Record. The form opens with the property, month and amount filled in. Pick how it was paid (Zelle, Avail, check…) and save. The row turns green.
  • 3Read the replies. Tenant texts show at the top of Rent Day under 💬 Replies with a one-line summary and a suggested answer. Reply, record, or mark done.
  • 4After the grace period: chase what's late. Late rows show the days late and the fee. Text from the row, or log a call so there's a record of what was said.
  • 5Clear "Still owed from earlier months". If an older month was actually paid, record it so it stops showing. A $0 record marks a vacant month.
  • 6Expenses and leases as they happen. Log an expense when you pay it (Expenses tab); lease alerts and to-dos sit further down Action Items.

3bNudging a tenant (Rent Day)

📣 Rent Day is the top of ✅ Action Items. Every occupied property is listed for the month with Paid / Partial / Due / Late, and each tenant has three buttons:

  • 💬Text — picks the right template (upcoming, due today, past due, balance, thanks), fills in the amount, due date, late fee and Zelle instructions, and opens Messages on your phone with it ready to send. Edit the wording first if you like.
  • ✉️Email — same thing, but opens Mail.
  • 📝Note — log a call or a conversation ("says Zelle coming Friday") so there's a record.

Everything you send is logged under the tenant, so neither of you has to ask "did anyone text them yet?". Tap a property to see that month's payments and messages.

First time: tap the ⚙️ on Rent Day and enter the Zelle instructions once. Due day, grace days and late fee live on each tenant (Tenants → edit) and default to the 1st, 5 days, no fee.
Twilio on? Then the main button is Send text (goes out from the Rainbow Rentals number) and tenant replies show up at the top of Rent Day under 💬 Replies, each with a 🦚 one-line read and a suggested answer. Tap Reply, check the wording, send. Scheduled reminders (before due, due day, after grace, then weekly) go out on their own at 10am — you'll get a push when they do.

7What each tab is for

On your phone, tap the section name at the top-left to switch tabs. You'll mostly live in Action Items — the rest are there when you need them:

✅ Action Items
Home base. Rent Day (who's paid, replies, reminders), expenses to review, lease alerts and to-dos.
🏠 Properties
Every property with its details, tenants, photos, and history. Tap one to drill in.
👤 Tenants
Every tenant with contact info, lease dates and rent terms. Paste list adds several at once.
💰 Income
The rent ledger — every payment recorded, by month and property.
💸 Expenses
Every expense logged, with receipts. Filterable by property and category.
⚖️ Reconcile
Recorded rents against actual bank deposits, month by month.
📊 Dashboard
Monthly money report — income vs. expenses per property.
📄 Documents
Leases, receipts, insurance docs — uploaded and searchable.

8One-time phone setup

📱 Add it to your home screen

  1. Open rainbowrentals.app in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
  2. Tap the Share button (iPhone: the square with the up-arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen → Add.

Now it opens like a regular app with the 🌈 icon.

🔔 Turn on reminders

Open the app from your home screen, find "🔔 Enable alerts on this device" under This Week on Action Items, and allow notifications. You'll get a push when a tenant texts back and when scheduled reminders go out.

1Getting started

Go to rainbowrentals.app and tap Sign in with Google. Use your own Google account — it's already on the approved list, so you'll land right on the app. Nobody else can get in.

  • 1Record each rent that came in. Tap the green Record button next to a property — the form opens already filled in with the right property, amount, and month. Just confirm (or tweak the amount if it was different) and save.
  • 2Update a lease — only if something changed (renewal, new rent amount, new tenant). Skip it most weeks.
  • 3Add expense — log anything you spent or got billed for: repairs, supplies, contractors. Do this right when it happens so nothing gets forgotten.
  • 4Tick the Avail checkbox once you've marked the rent payments in Avail too.
  • 5Check the to-dos. Tap Done on anything you finished this week.
  • 6Leave a note for Mike — anything worth sharing: a tenant called, something looks off, a question. More below in Leave a note.
  • 7Tap Done & send. This tells Mike you've finished the week's updates (and reminds him to pay you 😄).

Tap 🧾 Add expense on the weekly card (or the 💸 Expenses tab → add). Fill in what it was, pick the property, and attach a photo of the receipt if you have one:

Why it matters: every expense you log here goes straight into the tax records — so a 30-second entry now saves real money in April. When in doubt, log it and add a note.

8What each tab is for

On your phone, tap the section name at the top-left to switch tabs. You'll mostly live in Action Items — the rest are there when you need them:

✅ Action Items
Home base. The weekly update card, rents due, lease alerts, to-dos, and notes.
🏠 Properties
Every property with its details, tenants, photos, and history. Tap one to drill in.
👤 Tenants
All tenants in one list — contact info, lease dates, which property.
💰 Income
The rent ledger — every payment recorded, by month and property.
💸 Expenses
Every expense logged, with receipts. Filterable by property and category.
⚖️ Reconcile
Mike's view comparing recorded rents against actual bank deposits.
📊 Dashboard
Monthly money report — income vs. expenses per property.
📄 Documents
Leases, receipts, insurance docs — uploaded and searchable.

9One-time phone setup

📱 Add it to your home screen

  1. Open rainbowrentals.app in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
  2. Tap the Share button (iPhone: the square with the up-arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen → Add.

Now it opens like a regular app with the 🌈 icon.

🔔 Turn on reminders

Open the app from your home screen and tap "🔔 Enable weekly reminders on this device" on the weekly update card, then allow notifications. You'll get a nudge each week until you've tapped Done & send — after that it leaves you alone.

Questions? Text Mike — or leave a note in the app 📝

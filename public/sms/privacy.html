Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026 · Scope: tenant SMS communications

Rainbow Rentals, operated by Mike Dulin, MD, uses tenant contact information to manage rental communications. This policy explains how we handle information associated with our SMS program.

Information we collect

We collect your name, mobile number, rental property information, SMS consent records, messages you exchange with us, and message delivery and opt-out records. Messages may include information about rent, payments, maintenance, or other matters you choose to discuss with your property manager.

How we use information

We use this information to send requested rental communications, respond to questions, coordinate maintenance, record delivery, and honor communication preferences. SMS program messages concern your tenancy, including rent reminders, payment confirmations, maintenance scheduling, and property updates.

SMS information and sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Authorized property managers and service providers may process information as needed to operate the service. For example, Twilio and telecommunications carriers deliver messages, and our hosting and database providers support the application. This operational processing does not authorize those providers to market to you using your SMS consent. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect legal rights and the security of the service.

Your choices

Joining the SMS program is optional and is not a condition of renting. You may stop messages by replying STOP. We may send one confirmation of your opt-out. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact your property manager at mdulin@gmail.com. You can continue communicating through other available channels.

Retention and access

We retain communication and consent records as needed to operate the rental business, document consent and opt-out requests, and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Access is limited to authorized personnel and service providers. Contact us to request a correction or ask about access to or deletion of your information; some records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or honor an opt-out.

Questions and updates

For privacy questions, contact Rainbow Rentals / Mike Dulin, MD at mdulin@gmail.com. Updates to this policy will be posted with a revised date.

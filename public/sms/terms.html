SMS Terms

Rainbow Rentals, operated by Mike Dulin, MD, offers an optional tenant SMS program. After you provide consent, we may send automated and manager-written texts about rent reminders, payment confirmations, maintenance scheduling, and property updates. You may reply with questions for your property manager.

Consent and enrollment

Enrollment requires your affirmative SMS consent. Providing a mobile number for general contact does not by itself enroll you in this program. Consent is optional and is not a condition of renting. Keep your property manager informed if your mobile number changes.

Frequency and charges

Messages are generally sent a few times per month, with additional messages when a property issue needs attention or you are discussing a matter with your property manager. Routine messages are sent between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. local time; emergencies may require messages outside those hours. Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier’s plan determines those charges.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP to any program message to unsubscribe. You may receive one message confirming your opt-out. Reply START to opt back in, or contact your property manager to provide new SMS consent. Opting out does not change your lease obligations; your property manager can use the email address on your lease for reminders and notices.
